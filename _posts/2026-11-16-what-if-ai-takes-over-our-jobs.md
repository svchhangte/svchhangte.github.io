## What If AI Takes Over Most of Our Jobs?

One of the most popular topics among intellectuals, and something everyone should think about at least once, is how AI is gradually taking over human jobs these days.

Big companies are laying off more and more workers, something we seem to hear about almost every day.

## Why is AI replacing humans?

AI can now do most office work more efficiently than we humans can. A task that once required five people can now be done by just one expert working alongside AI, often faster and more reliably than five people working together until late in the evening.

Another advantage is that managing AI is much easier than managing humans. Additionally, the cost of hiring five people is much higher than subscribing to an AI model, especially when we consider the long-term expenses.

With all these advantages, companies that are among the first to adopt AI in their businesses and lay off employees are making more profits than those that still depend heavily on a human workforce.

## What if we all use AI and work more efficiently, so that we have an abundance of supplies that almost never runs out?

That is not necessarily how the world is going to work. If we all use AI and do the work that would otherwise require five people, that might sound like a good situation. We would still have jobs, and there would be plenty of products for us to consume.

But in reality, AI data centres may not be able to support such an enormous number of requests if everyone starts using AI at that scale. If we push beyond their current capabilities, the environmental damage caused by their development and operation could become unbearable.

## Then what if most of us are replaced by AI?

If most people on Earth were replaced by AI, with only a few human workers remaining, the unemployment rate could reach its highest level ever recorded.

Most of us would no longer be able to consume products because we would not be earning money. Companies might make more profits than ever before, but if people are not earning money, they cannot buy those products. Eventually, this could lead to a major economic downturn.

We don't know exactly what will happen in the future. But if AI takes over most of our jobs, there could be a huge gap between the employed and the unemployed, leading to even greater inequality in our societies.

I hope those who are in control will not allow this kind of crisis to happen. On the other hand, perhaps they already see what is coming but cannot control it. Or is this what they want? To control the world!

I hope that is not the case.

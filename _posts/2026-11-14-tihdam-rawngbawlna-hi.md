***TlHDAM RAWNGBAWLNA HI***

> **samm v. chhangte**

Mizo te hi thil mak leh danglam deuh buaipui chiam ṭhin leh heti lama hâwkdâk êm êm kan ni a tih theih àwm e.

Pathian thu tak thu Bible chhûng ngei aṭanga sermon ai chuan lungkuai zâwng(emotional) kàichhuak thiam thiam, a thawm ring ring leh maksak apiang hian ngaithlatu leh zuitu an ngah zêl mai.

**2 Tim 4:4: "an bengte chu thutak lamah an chhu ngawng ang a, thawnthu lam panin an pêng tawh ang."** tih thu hun hnuhnunga kohhran awm dan tùr ang deuh hian Bible thu tak tak buaipui lovin, a hrûl vêlah kan thle dual dual a nih tâk ber hi màw.

**Eng ngati nge Bible-ah Tihdam Rawngbawlna kan hmuh:**

Chanchin Ṭha(Gospel) hi Juda ho hnênah hrilh hmasak a ni a. Rom 1:16: ..**chhandamna tùra Pathian thiltihtheihna a ni si a; a tirah Juda mi tân a ni a..**

Juda ho hi Mizo te ang deuha thilmak làwm hnam an ni a. Thilmak(miracle) tihsak zet loh chuan an awih duh mai ṭhin lo. **1 Kor 1:22 "Juda-te chuan chhinchhiahnate an phût ṭhîn a, Grik-te chuan finna an zawng bawk ṭhîn a;"**

**Johana 4:48 "Tin, Isuan a hnênah, “Chhinchhiahnate leh thil makte in hmuh loh chuan in ring tawp lo vang,” a ti a."**

Chhinchhiahna leh thilmak an phût tlat avàngin, Pathian pawhin Thuthlung Hlui hun ata tawh thilmak tinrêng a tihsak fo ṭhin a, chuti chung chuan Pathian duh loh dànin an awm fo a ni. 

> **Kohhran Hmasa hunah Apostle te:**

Tichuan Kohhran hmasa ho kha a tam zâwk Juda an la ni a, Isua zirtirte a bik taka Tirhkoh nihna nei(Apostle)-te khân huaisen taka **Chanchin Ṭha** an puan theih nân leh an thute Juda-ten an lo awihsak theih nân thilmak an la tih chhunzawm a. Ram dangah pawh Chanchin Ṭha an puan zung zung theih nân Ram dang ṭawng tea ṭawng theihna an la nei a ni.

**2 Kor 12:12: "Tirhkoh nih chhinchhiahnate chu chhelna tinrêng neiin, chhinchhiahnate, thil makte, thiltihtheihnatein in zînga tih a nih tak kha."**

> **An la awih duh chuang lo**

Tirkohte Thiltih bung tâwp lamah khân, Juda luhlul takte chuan thilmak an hmu mawlh mawlh chungin Tirhkohte zirtirna chu an la pawm hleithei lo a. Rom khuaah *Paula* a awm laiin, zing aṭanga tlai thlengin thu an sawi a. (Tirhkohte 28 thla kha chhiar la).

Tichuan a ṭhenin an awih hrâm a, a ṭhenin an awih loa an inhnial a. Chutah Pauala chuan a kamkhat zui ta a nih kha. Isaian an luhlul zia a lo sawi tawh a dikzia tuihnih chungin a hau ta hlawm a. Tichuan a awih duh tùr hnam dang(Gentiles) hnenah Chanchin Ṭha an hril tawh tùr thu a sawi zui a nih kha.

**Tirhkohte 28:28,29 “Chutichuan, Pathian chhandamna thu hi Jentailte hnêna thawn a ni tih hre rawh u, anni zawngin ngaih pawh an ngaithla ang,” a ti a."**

Antioch Khuaah pawh:
**Tirhkohte 13:46 "Tin, Paula leh Barnaba chuan huai takin an sawi chhuak a, “Pathian thu hi nangmahni hnênah sawi hmasak tûr rêng a ni; nimahsela, in hnar kîr a, chatuana nung tlâk lova in inruat tâk ahnuin, hre rawh u, Jentailte lam kan hawi ta ve ang."**

> **Thilmak tih a reh ṭan**

Jentail-te lam an hawi hnuah Israel mai ni tawh lovin, mi zawng zawng hnênah Chanchin Ṭha chu puandarh a lo ni chho ta a. **Jentail-te chuan thilmak phût kher lovin kan lo awih ta hlawm a ni.**

Tirhkohte hun tâwp lamah pawh thilmak(miracle) a reh chho ṭan ta tih Bible-ah kan hmu:

**2 Timothea 4:20 "...Trophima erawh chu Mileti khuaah damlovin ka kalsan ta a."** Paulan tidam lovin a kalsan.

**1 Timothea 5:23:** Timothea pumna pawh tihdam sak mai lovin, uain te in zeuh zeuh tùrin a chah ta zâwk a ni. *(An hunlai hian wine hi dawidim pawlh a ni ve lo tih hriat erawh a ṭha, zu in fakmawi nân hman loh tur)*

**2 Korinth 12:7** thlaah pawh khan Puala vawi 3 lai a natna lakiang tùrin a ṭawngṭai a, **mahse Lalpan ka khawngaihna i tân a tâwk tiin a chhâng.**

A thui lutuk loh nân a tàwp lam hnaih tawh mai ila. 

Isua hian ral leh tùr taksa tihdamna lam ai chuan Thlarau damna(chatuana nunna) lam hi a hawi char char a. Châng lâkchhuah tùr a tam hlea hei hi dah mai ang: 

**Matthaia 10:28 “Tin, taksa tihluma thlarau tihlum thei lotute chu hlau suh ula; Gehenna-a thlarau leh taksa tiboral theitu chu hlau zâwk ang che u."**

**Johana 6:27 "Chaw dangral mai ûmin phe phe suh u; chatuana nun thlenga awm reng tûr chaw, Mihring Fapain a pêk tûr che u chu, ûmin phe zâwk rawh u; Pa Pathian chuan ani chu a chhinchhiah tawh si a,” a ti a, a chhâng a."**

Tun hunah hian kan buaipui ber tùr zâwk chu **Chatuan Nun** kan neihna tùr thlarau lam hi a ni zâwk a. Taksa damna ringawt buaipuia rawngbàwl hi a hun tawh lo a ni.

**Tin, Tirkoh(Apostle) te kha chuan za-ah-za(100%)-in dam lo an tidam a, dam thei lo palh vêl an awm ve lo ngai lo a ni. <i>Anni hun kha chu hun bîk a ni tlat!</i>**

**Setana power hmanga tihdamna kalpui pawh he khawvelah hian an awm ve tih kan hriat a ṭha:**
👇🏻
Matthaia 7:22 "Chumi niah chuan mi tam takin, ‘Lalpa, Lalpa, **i hmingin thute kan sawiin, i hmingin ramhuaite kan hnawt chhuakin, i hmingin thil mak tam tak kan ti ṭhîn lo vem ni?’** an la ti ang. Chumi hunah chuan, **‘Nangni thil sual tite u, ka hre ngai lo a che u, ka hnên ata kal bo rawh u,’ tiin anni chu ka la hrilh ang."**

**Tlângkawmna:**

1. Vawiinah hian Pathian hian dam lo a la tidam thei tehreng mai.

2. Ringtute pawh hian dam lo kan ṭawngṭai sak thei a, ṭawngṭai ruala kohhran ten dam lo kan ṭawngṭaisak te hi thil ṭha tak a ni.

3. 100% in an dam ang tih erawh a chiang tawh lo, Apostolic Period a zo tawh si a.

4. Pathianin a duh phawt chuan kan ṭawngṭaina a chhâng thei teh meuh mai; chutihrualin, a ruala taksa damna inkhâwm neiha intihdam pawp pawp erawh a tum ber a ni tawh lo tih Zoram mipuite hian kan hriat a ṭûl hle. 

5. Tihdam rawngbawlna kalpui vak vakte hian taksa damna leh hausakna(Prosperity Gospel) an thupui ber ṭhin a. Thlarau damna(Kraws thu) lam hi kan buaipui ber tur leh kan chhuan ber tùr a ni.

**Pathianin malsàwm che rawh se.** 🙏🏻✝️

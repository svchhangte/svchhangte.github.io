Kum 2015 kha kum 11 liam ta a lo ni ve leh reng tawh tih hriatna hian, mak deuhin lunglenna leh rilru hrehawm veng vengna min siam tlat mai pek a. Khâng hun te kha thlirkir chuan vawiina mihringte **dawhtheihna** a lo tlâkhniam tawh zia min hriatchhuah tir a ni.

Ni e, khâng hunah kha chuan technology tun angin a la sâng lova; chu chuan **dawhtheihna** min vawnhimsak tlat lawi si.

Internet a la muang em em a, mi tam zâwkin kan banphâk chu 2G network a la ni. A hnu deuhah khâng hun laia Internet Service Provider(ISP) company Mizorama lian tak tak **Airtel** leh **Vodafone** te khân 3G an rawn hawng ve ṭan êk êk chauh a la ni.

Facebook kha Internet kan khawih chhan tlânglâwn ber a ni mai a. **UC Browser** ngei mai te kha kan hmang ṭhin a. Post siam tùr leh comment pe tùra nghah rei a ngaih ṭhin zia te kha aw! Kan lo va **dawhthei** ṭhin em.

Kan hla duh zâwng tak mai download turte khân download theihna bîk website-ah kan han lût a, Mizo hla download tur chuan **zoland.in** te kan hmang ṭhin. Mp3 hla pakhat(~2MB) download tùra nghah rei a ngaih ṭhin zia te kha aw!

Online-a video en tùr lahin a in load chhûng a rei em em a, a vir chur chur kha kan en tam thei khawp mai.

Kan phone neih te kha **Nokia C2** tih te kha a ni hlawm a. Keypad type zâwnga hmeh rîk khep khep ngai a ni a(upa lam chuan *phone sik reng mai* an tih ṭhin phah). 😅 Z type tur chuan 9 awmna button kha vawi 4 hmeh te a ngai ṭhin. Chutiang zelin a dang pawh type chhûng a rei duh hle.

*Dawhtheihna* kha a hranpain kan zir pawh a ni lo a, kha kha khawvêl awmdàn phung ve rêng a ni.

Tichuan hun a lo kal deuh a. Internet te a lo chak ta hle a, tun hmaa WiFi connection kan pawh tirha 512kbps te chak kan lo tih ve ṭhin em em kha tunah chuan 100mbps te a lo ti ve ta dâwrh mai. Video pawh tâng awm miah lovin, HD quality pawh kan en zar zar theih tawh a. Engkim mai hi a awlsam chho ta a ni ber mai.

Tunah phei chuan **Artificial Intelligence(AI)** a lo chhuak leh tawh a. Thil a lo awlsam leh zual ta. Poster design tùr pawhin Photoshop hmanga darkar 1 design te kan peih ta meuh lo, AI kan siam tir mai a. Thusawi tur leh sermon turte pawh AI kan duan chhuah tir leh ta zêl mai bawk a. Engkimah kan thluak pawh kan hmang ṭha peih tawh lo ṭan a, engtin kan awm zêl ang ni maw?

Thatchhiatna leh dawhtheih lohna a kal kawp tlat a. Tunah zet zawng kan **dawhtheihna** te a lo tlahniam ta takzet a. Minute rei lo tê Internet chhia pawh kan mood tichhetu a ni chho ta!

Awlsamna leh changkànna hian mihring ze hlu tak zinga mi **dawhtheihna** zawi zawia a ṭhiah si chuan engtin nge hma kan lâk tâk ang?

1. Digital tel loa thil tih kan uar deuh deuh a ngai ta.
2. Lehkhapuana thil ziah kan uar chhoh leh a ṭul.
3. Movie en leh Podcast te ngaihthlâk ai chuan, **lehkhabu** chhiar uar thar leh a ngai a.
4. Pathian thu **Bible** aṭangin dawhtheihna zir thar a ṭul hlê bawk.

Nakinah kan chhunzawm leh dawn nia.. 🙏🏻
